## The keyboard nobody chose {fun-facts}

The QWERTY layout comes from the typewriter that Christopher Latham Sholes developed in the 1870s.

So every time you type a password, you are using a design older than the light bulb. Respect your elders.

## A bug with legs {discoveries}

In 1947, operators of the Harvard Mark Two computer found a moth stuck in a relay, and taped it into the logbook.

The note said first actual case of bug being found. Debugging used to mean tweezers.

## Coffee cam {quick-updates}

In 1991, researchers at the University of Cambridge pointed a camera at a coffee pot so they could see if it was empty without walking upstairs.

That is the internet in one sentence: incredible engineering, used to avoid a staircase.
